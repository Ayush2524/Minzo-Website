You can request deletion of your Minzo AI account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

01

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request by email from the address associated with your account:

Novare Talent Private Limited

Subject line: "Delete my Minzo AI account" — include the username or email your account is registered under.
  1. Email us from the address on your account, stating that you want your account deleted.
  2. We verify the request is coming from the account owner.
  3. We delete your account and data, and confirm by email once it's done.
Turnaround: requests are completed within 30 days, consistent with our Privacy Policy.
02

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your login credentials and authentication record.
  • Your profile — username, display name, avatar, and onboarding answers.
  • Your learning history — completed lessons, exercise answers, streaks, and per-concept progress.
  • Any prompts you wrote for graded exercises.

This action cannot be undone.

03

What we may retain, and for how long

A small amount of data may be kept briefly after deletion, strictly where required:

DataRetained forWhy
Server logs referencing your accountUp to 30 daysSecurity, abuse prevention
Records of the deletion request itselfUp to 12 monthsLegal/compliance record-keeping

No profile, learning-activity, or prompt content is retained past the deletion date. See our Privacy Policy for the full data-retention policy.

04

Deleting specific data instead of your whole account

If you'd rather keep your account but have specific data removed (for example, a single prompt submission), mention that instead in your email to the address above and we'll action just that request.
