RD SwapSo Pvt. Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") operates Minzo AI, a mobile application that teaches people to work well with AI tools. This policy explains what information we collect through the app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Minzo AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

01

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and a securely hashed password, if you sign up directly; or
  • Your name, email address, and profile photo from Google or GitHub, if you choose to sign in with one of those providers instead. We only receive what that provider's consent screen discloses to you at sign-in.

Authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password.

Profile information

You choose a username, a display name, and (optionally) an avatar. You can also add an optional bio, college, country and GitHub handle in Edit Profile. If you set a profile photo, the app asks for access to your camera (to take one) or your photo library (to choose one), only at that moment, and uploads just the single picture you select. Your username, display name, bio and avatar may be visible to other signed-in users of the app.

Onboarding information

During first-time setup, we ask for:

  • Your country (required) and your college or institution (optional).
  • Optionally, a phone number with its country code. We do not currently send a verification code to it, and you can skip this step. If we later require phone verification, we will ask you to confirm the number by SMS and update this policy first.
  • A self-reported answer to "how do you currently use AI" (e.g. conversation, tool, or system), used only to personalize which content is shown first.

Learning activity and content you create

As you use the app, we record which lessons and exercises you've completed, your answers, your streaks, and a per-concept "strength" score used to schedule spaced-repetition review. For exercises that ask you to write a real prompt, the text you write is stored and sent to our AI grading provider (see section 3) to be scored.

Reminders

If you turn on lesson reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device using your progress and streak. This happens on your device; we do not run a push-notification server and do not store a push token.

Subscriptions

If you buy Minzo Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status; we receive and store only your account identifier, whether your Pro access is active, when it ends, and which product it is. We never receive your card or payment details.

Diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, the app may send error reports (such as an error message, app version, device model and operating-system version) to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry. These reports are not used for advertising.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising SDKs and do not collect advertising identifiers. We do not access your contacts, microphone, or precise location. The camera and photo library are accessed only when you choose to set a profile picture.

02

How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and maintain your account and learning progress.
  • Personalize the order and difficulty of lessons shown to you.
  • Grade the "write a prompt" exercises using an AI service (see section 3).
  • Maintain streaks and spaced-repetition reminders.
  • Detect abuse and enforce rate limits on graded submissions.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.
03

Third parties we share data with

Minzo AI relies on the following service providers to operate. Each processes only the data necessary for its function, under its own privacy terms:

Supabase Infrastructure

Stores your account, profile, and learning-progress data, and handles sign-in. Supabase's own privacy policy governs its infrastructure-level processing.

OpenAI Grading

When you submit a "write a prompt" exercise, that text is sent to OpenAI's API to be scored and to generate feedback. No other exercise types are sent to OpenAI.

Google / GitHub Optional sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google or GitHub, those providers share the profile information you approve at their consent screen (typically name, email, and profile photo).

RevenueCat In-app purchases

Optional "Pro" subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and purchase status from the store so we can unlock Pro content. We do not receive or store your payment details.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

04

Data retention

We retain your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion (see section 6), we delete your profile, learning history, and exercise submissions within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain limited records for legal or security purposes.

05

Children's privacy

You must be at least 13 years old to use Minzo AI. If you are under 18 (or under the age of majority where you live), you should use the app with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us at awatadeayush2524@gmail.com and we will delete it.

06

Your rights and account deletion

Delete your account in the app: go to Profile and choose "Delete account". This permanently removes your account, profile, profile photo, learning progress and exercise submissions, and cannot be undone. You can also request deletion on our account deletion page if you can no longer sign in.

You may also request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by emailing awatadeayush2524@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will verify your request and act on it within 30 days. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, learning progress, and exercise submissions, and cannot be undone.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK) or state privacy laws (e.g. CCPA) — including the right to data portability and the right to object to certain processing. Contact awatadeayush2524@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

07

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers, and database-level access controls that ensure your data is only readable by you and, where applicable, other users viewing your public profile. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in countries other than your own. By using the app, you consent to your information being transferred to and processed in those countries, which may have different data protection laws than your home country.

09

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Effective date" at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision.
